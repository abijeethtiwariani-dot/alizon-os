Drop the ASAP curriculum sheet here (.xlsx, .xls or .csv). The system reads the format on its own — course name, hours, marks, every module, unit and topic — matches it to the course, and publishes it to the website and to each student’s dashboard. One sheet per course; a workbook with several sheets uploads them all. Re-uploading a course replaces its syllabus.
