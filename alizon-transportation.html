Getting Here & Around

Most of the programme is delivered online. When you do need to attend in person, this is how to reach Thiruvananthapuram and get around once you are here.

Most Alizon students never need to travel to us — the programme is delivered through ALIZON OS. When you do need to attend in person, for an induction, a viva or a workshop, this page covers how to reach Thiruvananthapuram and how to get around once you are there.

Reaching Thiruvananthapuram

By train

Thiruvananthapuram Central (TVC) is the main station and is served by long-distance and intercity services from across Kerala and beyond. Kochuveli (KCVL) handles a number of originating long-distance trains.

Central is the more convenient arrival point for the city, and the KSRTC bus terminal is immediately beside it.

By bus

The KSRTC bus terminal at Thampanoor sits adjacent to Thiruvananthapuram Central and receives state-run services from every district in Kerala, along with interstate routes.

Private long-distance operators also serve the city; arrival points vary by operator, so check your ticket rather than assuming Thampanoor.

By air

Trivandrum International Airport (TRV) serves domestic and international routes and is close to the city centre.

Prepaid taxis, app cabs and city buses all operate from the airport.

Getting around the city

City buses

KSRTC city services cover the main corridors and are the cheapest way to move around. Thampanoor is the principal interchange for city and long-distance routes alike.

Autorickshaws and app cabs

Autorickshaws are widely available. Agree the fare or ask for the meter before starting. App-based cabs and autos operate across the city and are usually the simplest option if you are arriving late or carrying luggage.

Planning your day

City traffic is heaviest through the morning and evening peaks. If you have a timed session — a viva, an examination or an induction — allow more time than the map suggests and aim to arrive early.

Accessibility. If you need step-free access, assistance on arrival, or any other adjustment for an in-person session, tell the office in advance and it will be arranged. Please do not wait until the day.
