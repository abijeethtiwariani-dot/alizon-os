Admission Notification

Official admission and allotment notifications issued by Alizon School of Medical & Digital Intelligence under the ASAP Kerala framework. Open a notification to read it in full or download it as a PDF.

Paste the notification

Write the notice anywhere — Word, Docs, an email — and paste the whole thing here. The system reads it on its own: 1. HEADING starts a section, tab- or pipe-separated pairs become a table, a) lines become a lettered list, the text before them is the intro and the text after is the note. Nothing needs tagging. Check what it recognised, then publish.

Or edit the fields directly

Everything below prints on the institution letterhead with the ASAP mark. Change the batch, dates, fees and seat counts for the next intake and publish — students and applicants see it immediately.
Corrections save as you type and apply to every document, not just this one.
Upload the seal once and every notification and experience letter carries it. Leave it out and the document simply prints no seal. You never need to come back here unless the seal changes.
