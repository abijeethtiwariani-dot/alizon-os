What makes it different

Most coaching sells volume. This sells diagnosis.

Ten thousand practice questions do not help a candidate who cannot tell which twenty of them they keep getting wrong for the same reason. Every MEDCITY batch is built around finding that out early and fixing it, and the timetable is arranged so that nothing new is taught in the last stretch before the exam.

Taught to the question pattern

Subjects are sequenced by what the paper actually weights and how it asks, not by the order they appear in a textbook.

Mocks under real conditions

Full length, real timing, real negative marking. A mock that is easier or kinder than the exam is worse than no mock.

An error log, not just a score

Every mock is followed by a written walkthrough, and each candidate keeps a running log of what went wrong and why.

Nothing new at the end

The final phase of every batch is consolidation and exam craft. New material that late helps nobody and frightens everybody.
