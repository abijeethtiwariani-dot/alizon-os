Your commission

The Drug and Therapeutics Committee has asked the drug information centre one question: “In adults with type 2 diabetes and chronic kidney disease, do SGLT2 inhibitors slow the progression of kidney disease compared with standard care?” You have one working day to produce a defensible evidence summary.

  1. Eight stages, each locked until the previous one is submitted — a review is reproducible only if done in order.
  2. Marks are earned: the 100 marks follow the assessment rubric below.
  3. A separate Evidence Integrity Index starts at 100% and falls whenever you let unsound evidence into the synthesis — a retracted paper, a fabricated citation, a misreported effect. It is scored independently of your marks.
  4. A 90-minute countdown runs from the moment you start. Unfinished stages score zero.
  5. You may consult the AI mentor three times in total.

Five badges are on offer — Search Strategist, Screening Expert, Bias Detective, Evidence Synthesiser and AI-Literate Pharmacist.
