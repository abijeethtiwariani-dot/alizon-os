Enterprise EHR & Hospital Information System Simulator

A working hospital information system on synthetic patients. Sign in as any of eight roles and the record changes shape around you: the front office sees demographics and never the clinical note, billing sees charges and cannot touch documentation. Move a patient through registration, encounter, orders, results, pharmacy, admission, bed allocation, insurance, discharge and referral — and watch every action you take land in the audit trail with your name on it.

Before you start

This is a records system, not a quiz. Role-based access is enforced: choosing the wrong role for a task will simply refuse you, and every attempt to open something your role cannot see is recorded in the audit trail exactly as it would be in a real HIS.

Two indices sit alongside your mark. Workflow integrity falls when a step is taken out of order — discharging before billing clearance, releasing a result that was never validated. Access discipline falls when you go looking in a record your role has no business in.

100% synthetic patients. Every patient, encounter, result, medication and charge in this simulator is generated for teaching. This is not a clinical system, it produces no diagnosis, no prescription and no treatment recommendation, and nothing in it may inform the care of a real person.
