Safety & Emergency

Emergency numbers for in-person sessions, how to keep your ALIZON AOS account secure, and how to raise a concern.

Alizon is a digital-first institution, so safety here means two things: knowing who to call in a physical emergency during any in-person session, and keeping your account, your records and your work secure online. Both are covered below.

In an emergency

These are the national and Kerala state emergency services. In a genuine emergency, call them first — before calling the institution.

ServiceNumberWhen to use it
All-in-one emergency112Police, fire or medical — the single national emergency number
Ambulance108Emergency medical response and transport
Fire & rescue101Fire, rescue and related emergencies
Women's helpline1091Emergency support for women
Child helpline1098Emergency support for a child in distress
Anti-ragging helpline1800-180-5522Ragging or harassment at any educational institution in India
Cyber crime1930Financial fraud and cyber crime reporting
At an in-person session, tell the session coordinator immediately after emergency services have been called, so the institution can assist and account for everyone present.

Keeping your account safe

Your login is yours alone

Your registration number and password identify you in every assessment record, attendance entry and certificate we issue. Sharing them means someone else's work can be recorded as yours — and yours as theirs.

Change your password from your profile if you think anyone else has it.

How we will and will not contact you

Official communication comes from the institution's own channels and from Info@alizon.in.

We will never ask for your password, and we will never ask you to pay fees to a personal bank account or UPI ID. If a message does either, it is not from us — report it before acting on it.

Your records

Your submissions, results and attendance are held to support your assessment and certification. If you believe a record is wrong, raise it through the portal rather than working around it.

Verify a certificate →

Conduct, and raising a concern

Alizon expects students and staff to treat each other with respect, online and in person. Ragging, harassment, discrimination and abusive conduct are not tolerated in any Alizon session, forum or channel, and neither is impersonation or submitting another person's work as your own.

Raise it with us

Every student can raise an enquiry or a grievance from inside the portal, and it reaches the administration directly.

Portal → Faculty & Support →

Raise it externally

You are entitled to go outside the institution. The national anti-ragging helpline is 1800-180-5522, and cyber crime including online financial fraud is reported on 1930.

If you are unsure

Raise it anyway. It is better to report something that turns out to be nothing than to leave something that turns out to matter.
