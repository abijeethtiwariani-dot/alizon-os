Getting Around

Campus & Locations

Alizon is a digital-first institution based in Thiruvananthapuram, Kerala. Here is what happens online, what happens in person, and how to reach us.

Alizon is a digital-first institution. Almost everything a student does with us — practicals, examinations, textbooks, assessment and results — happens inside ALIZON OS, from wherever they are. That is deliberate: it is how a working pharmacist in Kannur and a student in Thiruvananthapuram can sit the same practical on the same standard. This page sets out what happens online, what happens in person, and how to reach us when you need a person rather than a portal.

What happens online

  • All virtual practicals and AI-scored labs
  • Module textbooks and the digital library
  • Assignments, self-assessments and practical reports
  • Internal examinations and mock tests
  • Results, certificates and verification

What happens in person

  • Induction and orientation sessions, where scheduled
  • Practical verification and viva at the centre allotted to you
  • Workshops and guest sessions announced on the events page
  • On-the-job training at partner sites, for programmes that include it

Where we are

The institution is based in Thiruvananthapuram, Kerala. Alizon operates as an initiative under ASAP Kerala — the Additional Skill Acquisition Programme of the Department of Higher Education, Government of Kerala — and is registered with the Kerala Startup Mission.

Centres and partner sites vary by programme and batch. The allotment issued with your admission tells you which centre applies to you.
