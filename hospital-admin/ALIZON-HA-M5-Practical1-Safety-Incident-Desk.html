Patient Safety Incident Desk, RCA & Risk Register

You staff the Quality & Patient Safety cell at ALIZON SmartCare. Six incident reports came in overnight, each pre-classified by a triage engine that grades on the harm that reached the patient. You decide what actually happened, how deep the investigation goes, and what enters the risk register — and the report where nobody was hurt is not automatically the small one.

Before you start

Each report opens with the triage engine's harm grade. The full report stays closed until you open it, and what you opened is recorded. You may dispose of an incident on the harm grade alone — that is exactly how a recurring system fault gets closed as a one-off.

Two indices sit alongside your mark. Systemic risk captured falls when a fault that will recur is closed locally without reaching the register. Reporting culture falls when an individual is disciplined for a failure the system set them up for — punish the person and the next incident simply goes unreported.

Educational simulation on synthetic data. Every incident, patient, drug and staff member here is invented for teaching. Nothing describes a real event or a real hospital, and nothing this system outputs is clinical advice or a real safety determination.
