Hospital Administration AI · Module 3 · Practical 1

AI Patient Experience, Feedback & Service-Recovery Command Centre

You are the Patient Relations Officer at ALIZON SmartCare. Complaints, feedback forms, NPS verbatims, call transcripts and public reviews all arrive here. An AI layer classifies each one before you see it. Your job is to decide whether its reading is right, correct it where it is not, write the reply that actually goes out, and turn a pile of individual complaints into the systemic finding management needs.

Before you start

Every case carries an AI analysis with nine fields. You must judge each field Agree or Disagree before you can close the case — and on several of these cases the AI is confidently wrong in a way that matters. Accepting its classification unchanged is recorded.

Two indices sit alongside your mark. Service recovery falls when a case is closed without the action it needed. Communication integrity falls when a reply you send discloses something it should not — and one of these cases is a public review, where confirming that someone is a patient at all is itself a disclosure.

Educational simulation on synthetic data. Every complaint, review, transcript and score here is written for teaching. Nothing describes a real person or a real hospital, and no output of this system is clinical advice.
