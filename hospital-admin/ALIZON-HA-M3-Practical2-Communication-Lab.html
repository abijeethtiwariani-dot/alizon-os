Hospital Administration AI · Module 3 · Practical 2

Healthcare Communication, Conflict Management & Patient Interaction Lab

Four conversations that go differently depending on what you say. An angry patient, a family that was never told how discharge works, two departments each certain the other is wrong, and a colleague who has had enough. The other person responds to your actual choices — interrupt them and they escalate, solve before you have understood and they tell you that you have not listened.

Before you start

Each conversation tracks five stages — acknowledge, clarify, explain, solution, confirm — and a tension level that rises and falls with what you say. Offering a solution before you have acknowledged the concern and clarified the facts is the most common failure in hospital communication, and here it produces the response it produces in real life.

There is no single scripted path. The other person's next line is chosen from what you just did and how tense the conversation already is, so the same opening can end four different ways.

Educational simulation. Every character and situation is written for teaching. No real person is depicted, and nothing here is clinical advice. This lab runs entirely in your browser — the responses come from a rule-based conversation model built into the page, not from a live language model.
