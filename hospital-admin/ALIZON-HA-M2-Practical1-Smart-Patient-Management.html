You are the Smart Hospital Patient Services & Administration Officer at ALIZON SmartCare Multispecialty Hospital. One morning of the outpatient operation is yours: the queues, the appointment book, the patients who did not arrive, and the ones who have been waiting far too long to be told nothing. Find what is actually causing the congestion, decide what to do about it, and say the right thing to the people waiting.

Before you start

This is a service-coordination console, not a chatbot. Every department queue stays closed until you open it, and what you opened is recorded. The AI will give you an analysis when you ask for one — it is a starting point for your own reading of the data, not a verdict, and on this shift it is not entirely right.

Two indices sit alongside your mark. Service grip falls when you act on the wrong constraint or leave an escalation too late. Communication integrity falls when a message you send to a waiting patient discloses something it should not, alarms them unnecessarily, or leaves them without a next step.

Educational simulation on synthetic data. Every patient, token, appointment and no-show score in this laboratory is generated. Nothing here describes a real person, and no output of this system may be used to make a decision about an individual patient in real healthcare.
