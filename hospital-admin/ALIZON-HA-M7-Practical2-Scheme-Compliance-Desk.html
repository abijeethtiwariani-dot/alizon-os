Pre-Authorisation, PM-JAY & ABDM Compliance Desk

You staff the insurance and scheme help desk. Six packets are open — cashless pre-authorisations, a scheme claim, a consent record and a payment that should never have been taken. Some will be paid, some will be returned, and one of them is a matter for the hospital rather than the insurer.

Before you start

Each packet opens with the desk summary. The documents behind it stay closed until you open them, and what you opened is recorded. A packet submitted on its summary is a packet nobody read.

Two indices sit alongside your mark. Claim outcome falls when a payable claim is abandoned or a defective one is pushed through to certain rejection. Scheme compliance falls when a submission or a payment breaches the terms the hospital accepted on empanelment — including the term that a scheme beneficiary is not asked for money.

Educational simulation on synthetic data. Every patient, packet, policy, scheme reference and figure is invented for teaching. Scheme and ABDM behaviour is simplified for the exercise and is not a statement of current rules. Nothing here is legal, insurance or billing advice.
