Clinical Documentation Integrity & EMR Records Desk

You run the Medical Records Department at ALIZON SmartCare for one working morning. Six charts are queued for audit before the Health Information Management committee sits at noon. An AI documentation engine has already scored each one. Your job is to decide whether its reading survives contact with the actual chart — and a record that reads as complete is not the same thing as a record that is safe.

Before you start

The AI score is visible immediately. The chart itself stays closed until you open it, and which charts you opened is recorded. A verdict reached without opening the chart is a verdict reached on the engine's word.

Two indices sit alongside your mark. Record integrity falls when a defective chart is accepted, and also when a sound chart is returned — clinician time is not free. Legal defensibility falls when a chart carrying a legal defect is passed to the committee: an unsigned entry, a late entry with no addendum marker, or an amendment with no audit trail behind it.

Educational simulation on synthetic data. Every patient, note, timestamp and identifier here is invented for teaching. Nothing describes a real person or a real hospital, and nothing this system outputs is clinical advice or a real coding determination.
