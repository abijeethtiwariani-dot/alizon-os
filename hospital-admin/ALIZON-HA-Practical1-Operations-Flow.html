Before you start

Every stage of the patient journey stays closed until you open it, and what you opened is recorded. Deciding without looking costs you.

Two indices sit alongside your mark: Operational grip, which falls when you act on the wrong bottleneck, and Evidence discipline, which falls when you commit to a decision without opening the data that would have shown it.

The simulator produces operational estimates, not clinical predictions. Nothing it outputs may be used to decide anything about an individual patient.
