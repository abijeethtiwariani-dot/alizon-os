Revenue Cycle, Leakage & Denial Management Desk

You run the revenue cycle desk at ALIZON SmartCare. Six items are open: money earned and never billed, claims returned by insurers, and suggestions from the AI billing assistant about how to recover more. Two of those suggestions would work. Both are fraud. The month's recovery target is real, and so is the line you are not allowed to cross to reach it.

Before you start

Each item opens with the amount at stake and what the billing system says about it. The underlying record stays closed until you open it, and what you opened is recorded.

Two indices sit alongside your mark. Net revenue position tracks money correctly recovered or correctly returned. Compliance integrity falls when revenue is recovered by a route the documentation does not support — and it falls hardest on the two items where taking the money is easy, defensible-sounding and unlawful.

Educational simulation on synthetic data. Every patient, claim, tariff, code and rupee figure here is invented for teaching. Nothing describes a real hospital, insurer or scheme, and nothing this system outputs is billing, coding, legal or financial advice.
