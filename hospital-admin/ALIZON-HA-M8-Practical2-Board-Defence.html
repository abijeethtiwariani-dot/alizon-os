Case Analysis & Board Defence

You have submitted a recommendation. Now you sit in front of the board that has to fund it. Five challenges are coming — one of them is correct and you will have to concede it, one is a misreading you must correct, and one is an offer of an easy way out that costs more than it saves.

Before you start

Read your own recommendation and the evidence behind it before the board convenes. Once a challenge is put to you there is no going back to check.

Two indices sit alongside your mark. Credibility falls when you over-claim, guarantee what cannot be guaranteed, or defend a point the evidence does not support. Substance falls when you give ground you did not have to give — deferring to seniority, or abandoning a sound position because it was challenged confidently.
