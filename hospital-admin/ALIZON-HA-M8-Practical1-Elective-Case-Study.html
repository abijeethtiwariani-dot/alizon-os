Elective Case Study Desk

This module is elective. Choose the area you intend to work in, and you will be handed a real administrative case from it — three decisions deep, with the evidence released only as you ask for it. Every case turns on something the headline number does not say.

Before you start

You choose one elective. That choice is recorded on your report, because the module is assessed on depth in a chosen area rather than breadth across all six.

Two indices sit alongside your mark. Analytical rigour falls when a conclusion is drawn before the evidence that would test it has been opened. Judgement falls when the action does not follow from what the evidence actually showed.

Educational simulation on synthetic data. Every hospital, figure, patient and staff member here is written for teaching. Nothing describes a real organisation, and no output of this system is clinical, legal or financial advice.
