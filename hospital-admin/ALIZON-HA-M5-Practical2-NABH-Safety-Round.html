Before you start

Each stop opens with what you can see from the doorway. The detailed inspection stays closed until you carry it out, and what you inspected is recorded. Scoring an area on appearance is exactly what a self-assessment is meant to prevent.

Two indices sit alongside your mark. Patient safety risk falls when a real hazard is scored compliant. Accreditation readiness falls when a finding is graded below what the evidence supports — and fabricated documentation is a major non-conformity, not a paperwork slip.

Educational simulation on synthetic data. Every ward, register, staff member and finding here is invented for teaching. Nothing describes a real hospital or a real assessment, and this exercise is not an NABH assessment, a legal opinion, or a real compliance determination.
