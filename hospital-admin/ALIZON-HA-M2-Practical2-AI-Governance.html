AI Hospital Administrator Intelligence & Responsible AI Decision System

You are the Hospital Administrator and AI Governance Officer at ALIZON SmartCare. An AI layer now sits across administration and it produces recommendations faster than anyone can check them. Your job is not to use the AI. Your job is to decide, case by case, whether the hospital should accept it, change it, supervise it, or refuse it — and to be able to say why.

Before you start

Every AI recommendation in the inbox must be evaluated on six dimensions before you are allowed to decide on it. That is deliberate: an administrator who accepts a recommendation without knowing what it was built from has not made a decision, they have forwarded one.

Two indices sit alongside your mark. Governance integrity falls when you accept an unsafe recommendation, mishandle patient data, or let an unverified claim into an official report. Evidence verification falls when you decide without opening the data behind the recommendation.

Educational simulation on synthetic data. Every dataset, alert, confidence figure and cost in this laboratory is generated for teaching. Nothing here is a real hospital record, a real financial forecast, or advice about any individual patient.
