Hospital KPI Validation & MIS Board Pack

You are the MIS analyst at ALIZON SmartCare and the board pack goes out on Friday. Six indicators have arrived from the departments, each with the raw numbers behind it. Recompute them, decide which mean what they appear to mean, and decide what the board is told — because the fastest way to mislead a board is with a figure that is arithmetically correct.

Before you start

Each indicator opens with the figure the department reported. The raw data behind it stays closed until you open it, and what you opened is recorded. An indicator signed off without the raw numbers is an indicator taken on trust.

Two indices sit alongside your mark. Data integrity falls when a misleading figure reaches the board. Decision usefulness falls when a sound figure is withheld or escalated — a board pack that reports nothing until everything is perfect is not cautious, it is useless.

Educational simulation on synthetic data. Every dataset, department and figure here is invented for teaching. Nothing describes a real hospital, and no output of this system is a real performance, clinical or governance determination.
