Hospital Administration AI · Module 6 · Practical 2

Predictive Bed Planning & the AI Decision-Support Desk

Six outputs are waiting on your desk: forecasts from the hospital's predictive models and drafts from its generative AI assistant. Every one of them is fluent, confident and instantly usable. Your job is to decide which deserve to be acted on, which need checking against the source first, and which the assistant should never have been asked to produce at all.

Before you start

Each item opens with the AI output as it was handed to you. The source data and model detail stay closed until you open them, and what you opened is recorded. Acting on a fluent draft without checking its source is the specific failure this practical exists to teach.

Two indices sit alongside your mark. Factual integrity falls when an unverified or fabricated output is used. Decision quality falls when a sound model is overridden — an administrator who rejects every forecast has bought an expensive system and disabled it.

Educational simulation on synthetic data. Every model, dataset, draft and figure here is invented for teaching. Nothing describes a real hospital, and this system produces no clinical advice and no real forecast.
