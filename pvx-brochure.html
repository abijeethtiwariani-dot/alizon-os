AI ADR Detective

Interview. Investigate. Identify. Report.
● Free · open to everyone · no account needed

You are not given a case.
You are given a patient who has noticed something — and nothing else.

They answer only what you actually ask. Ask nothing about the dose, and you will not learn the dose. Everything else you must go and find: the treating doctor, the dispensing record, the medical notes, the laboratory. Then decide what the reaction was, whether it is serious, whether the medicine caused it, and what the safety team should do about it.

05Who it is for

Pharmacy students at any stage, practising pharmacists, nurses, and clinicians who want to work a real pharmacovigilance investigation rather than read about one. No prior experience of adverse drug reaction reporting is assumed — Level 1 teaches the shape of a case from the beginning. Anyone already confident can start at Level 4 or above.

06How to start

Open it on any browser
alizon.in/ALIZON-OS-Workshop-PVX

Scan the code or type the address. Give your name, roll number and phone at the door — that is the whole sign-in — and choose a case. Nothing you do inside the workshop is submitted anywhere unless you are a signed-in Alizon student.

07Questions students ask

QuestionAnswer
Do I need an account?No. Your name, roll number and phone at the start, and nothing else.
Does it cost anything?No. It is free and stays free.
Can I stop and come back?Finish a case in one sitting — about an hour. Your sign-in is remembered, so the next case starts straight away.
What do I get at the end?A marked performance report across ten competencies, with the hidden case revealed and specific feedback on what you missed. You download it as a PDF and keep it.
Is it marked?Yes, out of 100. Alizon students can submit their report to faculty; everyone else keeps theirs.
Do I need to be an Alizon student?No. The workshop is open to everyone.
Will it work on my phone?Yes. A larger screen is easier for the database work in the later cases.
