Student Life at Alizon

What student life actually looks like at a working, digital-first institution — and how to take part.

Alizon is a working institution rather than a residential campus: students are pharmacists, nurses, administrators and graduates spread across Kerala and beyond, studying alongside their jobs. Student life here is built around the cohort, the sessions and the work itself rather than around clubs and grounds. This page sets out what actually exists and how to take part.

Your batch

You are admitted into a batch and you move through the programme with it. Batch communication — induction, schedules, assessment windows and announcements — runs through the portal and the official channels named in your admission letter.

Workshops & guest sessions

Live sessions, inductions and guest lectures are announced through the portal and listed publicly. These are where a cohort actually meets, and attendance is recorded.
